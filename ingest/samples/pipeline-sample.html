Nitrogen application

Apply half of the recommended nitrogen at sowing together with the full dose of phosphorus and potash. Split the remaining nitrogen between the first irrigation at crown root initiation and the tillering stage. Use a leaf colour chart before the last split and skip it when leaves are already dark green.

  • Prefer neem-coated urea in split doses to reduce losses.
  • Avoid top-dressing when heavy rain is forecast within two days.
